# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge and standards

Acorn adopts the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html). Everyone participating in Acorn—human contributors, reviewers, the maintainer, and anyone directing an AI agent—is expected to keep the project welcoming and harassment-free.

- Direct technical, mathematical and scientific criticism at the work (the specification, theorem, proof, algorithm, claim or document), never at the person.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, and accept constructive criticism of claims, proofs and designs.
- Do not engage in harassment, personal or political attacks, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all spaces managed by the Acorn project on GitHub ([issues](https://github.com/rbeauchamp/acorn/issues), [pull requests](https://github.com/rbeauchamp/acorn/pulls), reviews, comments, and [Discussions](https://github.com/rbeauchamp/acorn/discussions) when enabled) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Acorn is maintained by one owner, Richard Beauchamp ([@rbeauchamp](https://github.com/rbeauchamp)), together with the organization's autonomous agents.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through [GitHub's private reporting for rbeauchamp/acorn](https://github.com/rbeauchamp/acorn/security/advisories/new) (Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability, which reaches only the repository owner; see [SECURITY](SECURITY.md)). The owner reviews reports with the assistance of the project's agents (targeting initial review within seven days, matching the repository's [triage cadence](CONTRIBUTING.md#triage-and-response)) and keeps the reporter's identity confidential.

To enforce these standards, the owner may warn a participant, edit, remove or close comments, issues, commits or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, or temporarily or permanently block an account from the repository.
