# Architectural foundation: 4-layer federation and always-on live upgrades

**Status:** Technical architecture and decision record derived from [the architectural intent](architectural-intent.md) against codebase forensics at baseline commit *9aea3cb* (branch *main* has since upgraded to Lean v4.36.0 and Regula v0.13.1 via PR #334 at commit *d500290*, so Phase 0.1–0.2 target that active toolchain and re-verify the historical v4.35.0 / v0.13.0 line anchors below against v4.36.0). Owner-confirmed directions live in [the architectural intent](architectural-intent.md); the design, package boundaries, compiler recipes, and governance mechanics below are delegated engineering choices designed to uphold those commitments and [the mission contract](mission-contract.md).

## 1. Purpose, scope, and authority boundary

Acorn's mission is to discover and implement algorithms that enable agents to achieve goals in big worlds, verified in Lean 4 from a single experience stream ([README](../README.md#purpose-and-worlds)). Two requirements established on 2026-11-21 govern this architectural foundation:

1. **Community swarm scale (100–1,000+ concurrent contributors):** Pointing hundreds or thousands of external human and AI-agent swarms at Acorn must produce compounding verified progress rather than merge storms on central registry files, 360-second stage verification deadlines, or a dead-code hairball inside the released agent.
2. **Always-on living operation and author-agnostic RSI:** The agent must operate as a fault-tolerant, never-pausing system supporting verified zero-downtime live upgrades across an arbitrarily long lifetime — starting with externally pushed verified releases and designed from day one so future recursive self-improvement (RSI) modules pass through the exact same immutable outer verification and upgrade gate.

Owner-confirmed commitments live in [architectural intent](architectural-intent.md). Everything below specifies the concrete engineering architecture, compiler guarantees, anti-gaming closures, operational supervision, and phased GitHub issue roadmap ([epic #321](https://github.com/rbeauchamp/acorn/issues/321)) implementing those commitments.

## 2. Codebase forensics at baseline 9aea3cb

Inspecting Acorn's 399 maintained Lean source files at commit 9aea3cb established where monolith pressure actually sits versus which modular seams already exist.

### 2.1 Artificial centralization vs. real coupling bottlenecks

1. **Decision registries (`lean/Acorn/Decisions.lean` and `lean/AcornVerif/Decisions.lean`):** `Acorn.Decisions` is 8,462 lines / 477 KB (591 written theorem declarations, including 231 *Regula.RegisteredDecision* declarations and 277 *Regula.ExecutableContract* declarations) and has **zero importers anywhere in the repository**. Its single-module status and native-compilation exemption are defined by *AcornModuleInventory.decisionRegistry* and *AcornModuleInventory.nativeModule* in `lean/AcornTools/ModuleInventory.lean` and enforced by literal `Acorn.Decisions` checks in `lean/AcornTools/Ownership.lean` (*AcornOwnership.anchors*), `lean/AcornTools/Boundary/Audit.lean` (*AcornBoundaryAudit.registry* and *AcornBoundaryAudit.importAllowed*), and `lean/AcornTools/OwnershipAudit.lean` (*AcornOwnershipAudit.contractOwners* and *AcornOwnershipAudit.registrations*), with that centralization documented in [the verification guide](verification.md#regula-audit). Sharding it into per-module leaf files is purely mechanical once those inventory/audit rules accept leaf suffixes.
2. **Ten central merge-conflict files:** Adding or changing a learning mechanism today forces concurrent PRs onto up to ten shared files: `lean/Acorn/Decisions.lean`, `lean/AcornVerif/Decisions.lean`, `lean/Acorn/Cost.lean` (a closed 92-constructor `Site` enum at line 238), `lean/AcornTools/Native/Routes.lean` (334 C-mangled symbol call routes and 14 closure lists), `lean/AcornTools/Native/Resources.lean`, `lean/AcornTools/Ownership.lean` (386 anchors and 20 executables), `lean/AcornTools/Boundary/Departures.lean`, `lean/AcornTools/AxiomPins.lean` (4,239 lines), `lean/AcornVerif/Landings.lean`, and `lean/AcornVerif/Targets/LearnedStateAcorn.frame` (2,147 declaration hashes).
3. **Closed-enum research profiles and manual destructuring:** Acorn uses zero typeclasses for agent subsystem dispatch. `Host.Runner` and `Handcrafted.FeatureProfile` map closed research-profile enums across dependent type indices and inline branches inside 5,178 lines of `lean/Acorn/Handcrafted/` (`TemporalControl`, `StepParts`, `DrawFirst`, `AgentAlignment`, `AgentEpisodes`, `Agent`), manually pattern-matching 5-level nested tuples so Lean 4 reference-count-1 in-place array updates hold across `detachedUpdate` and `takeOut` (`lean/Acorn/FeatureConsumers.lean`).
4. **Two-stage 360 s verification deadlines and Regula blast radius:** Under `scripts/gate.sh` (lines 68–79), `scripts/verify.sh` runs two independent 360-second verification stages (executing build then full verification), followed outside both deadlines by `scripts/regula.sh` replaying all claimed modules under `Acorn` and `AcornVerif` in one cone. Editing core files (`State`, `SwiftTd`, `Features`) dirties ~66% (126–133 of 196) of `AcornVerif`; editing `Handcrafted` control dirties ~32% (60–65 of 196). Earlier mechanism PAR-18 had to be retired partly because its proofs sat on the critical path of the build budget ([prior-art review](prior-art-review.md)).
5. **Baseline-pinning invalidation:** Acorn's first plugin experiment ([the learned-state frame](learned-state-frame.md)) demonstrated that allowing contributors to replace the agent's step broke ablation guarantees (revision 1), forcing revision 2 to pin the baseline agent by declaration hash (`lean/AcornVerif/Targets/LearnedStateAcorn.frame`) — meaning baseline edits altering declarations reached by the pinned frame (`lean/AcornTools/Frame.lean`:193–217, 2,147 declarations) change the pin digest and fail frame admission until re-pinned (earlier theorems remain proved of their earlier definitions, and proof-only edits inside a pinned theorem leave the digest unchanged).

## 3. Architectural evaluation and primary-source foundations

Four candidate architectures were compared as engineering designs under Acorn's current Lean compiler, Regula, and gate constraints (assessments reflect practical gate/toolchain fit rather than mathematical impossibility theorems):

| Criterion (100–1,000+ contributors) | Model A: Dynamic runtime plugin bus in Lean | Model B: Monorepo coarse plugins only | Model C: Whole-agent competition islands only | Model D (Adopted): 4-Layer Federation + Always-On Supervisor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lean kernel / Regula / FBIP fit** | Blocked under current rules (dynamic lookup, open axioms, indirect calls break borrowing) | Admitted | Admitted | Admitted (*module* opacity + explicit specialization + emitted-IR/exclusivity audits) |
| **OaK cyclic feedback loops** | Poor fit (mutually requiring components inactive; densely coupled cycles bloat) | Strong inside one fixed wiring | Strong per agent | Strongest (coarse A/G + RUSC seams inside baseline; free internals in novel agents) |
| **Genuine algorithmic discovery** | Constrained by runtime hooks | Constrained (fixed FC-STOMP slots) | Unrestricted | Unrestricted (supports both surgical seam swaps and paradigm shifts) |
| **Compounding of discoveries** | Weak (shallow contracts) | High inside one wiring | Poor (isolated islands) | High (distillation into primitives + multi-axis synthesis missions) |
| **Merge contention & 360 s verification** | Severe (shared registries + global cone) | High (1 global cone + central files) | O(1) in swarm count (candidate size/complexity fixed) | O(1) in swarm count (candidate size/cone fixed; conditional on cache hygiene) |
| **Baseline cleanliness (Anti-Frankenstein)** | Worst (hook/flag accumulation) | Moderate (profile/flag creep) | High | Highest (one clean epochal baseline; replace-and-delete promotion; simplification missions) |
| **Always-on fault isolation & live upgrades** | Unisolated (shared process; no verified migration) | Requires restart | Requires restart | Designed natively (OS-process isolation + quiescent symmetric-lens migration + canary rollback) |

### 3.1 Why a dynamic Cordis-style runtime bus is rejected inside OaK (and no separate framework repo upfront)

Direct inspection of Cordis's TypeScript core and its 92-page formalization (Shi, Zhang & Cui, *A Programming Paradigm for Spatiotemporal Composability*, arXiv:2608.25512), DeepSeek-Harness's architecture and 30+ vendored framework patches, and Lean v4.35.0's compiler/Regula constraints establishes why an in-process dynamic plugin bus is not adopted for Acorn's continual-learning core:

1. **Pure static realization in Lean (arXiv:2608.25512 §1.1, Def. 23, §6.7):** Cordis Definition 23 compares two realizations of an effect inside a mutable JS/TS host process. In Lean's statically compiled pure setting without an ambient mutable runtime registry, choosing static module/record wiring is an engineering fit to Lean's kernel and verification pipeline rather than relying on dynamic runtime registration.
2. **Incompatibility with Lean 4 kernel rules and borrow/ABI efficiency:** Lean v4.35.0's runtime dynamic type carriers (*Dynamic*) rely on `unsafe` casts forbidden inside Regula-claimed declarations under RG1006 (which governs individual declarations in the claim, not indirection as a class); open extensible families (Lake.Util.Family) rely on explicit axioms forbidden inside the claim under RG1001; elaboration hooks (*initialize*, macros, custom attributes) are banned by `AcornTools.Boundary.Audit`; and indirect `.fvar` closure calls mark all arguments owned in LCNF (*Lean/Compiler/LCNF/InferBorrow.lean*:403–405), preventing borrowed `@&` signatures and unboxed scalar ABIs across calls (forcing reliance on last-use exclusivity for in-place array updates, where `detachedUpdate` in `lean/Acorn/FeatureConsumers.lean` notes runtime exclusivity rather than call directness alone governs whether arrays mutate in place or copy).
3. **Hostile topology for OaK and fault isolation:** Cordis forbids dependency cycles (arXiv:2608.25512 §4.1, §6.5 — mutually requiring components remain inactive, and §6.5 notes factoring *densely interacting* components can require quadratically many integration components), breaks commutativity on ordered middleware waterfalls (§3.4.2), carries only nominal key identity without behavioral invariants (§6.6), and executes in a shared process heap — matching Armstrong's Erlang/OTP fault-isolation principle (Armstrong PhD 2003, Ch. 2.3) that shared-memory plugins cannot isolate faults or deadline overruns. Choosing static coarse algebraic seams is an Acorn engineering choice grounded in those constraints.
4. **No separate standalone composition framework repository upfront:** Building a generic Lean composition framework in a separate repository invites premature category-theoretic abstraction decoupled from Lean v4.35.0 FBIP and Regula rules. Instead, minimal unboxed composition structures (~300–600 LOC) live directly inside `acorn-contracts`, with an explicit **graduation trigger**: extract them into a standalone library only after at least two substantially distinct verified agent architectures stabilize them across at least two epochs and a second Regula-compatible project requires them.

### 3.2 Synthesis of primary literature adopted

- **RL modularity history and OaK epistemology:** RL framework history (TorchRL, ICLR 2024 cataloging nine unmaintained frameworks; RLlib's multi-class rewrite; Dopamine and CleanRL showing premature internal splits obscure coupled updates and block radical ideas) and Sutton, Bowling & Pilarski's *Alberta Plan* (arXiv:2208.11173v3, *"the dilemma of the parts and the whole"*) show that internal agent decompositions are research hypotheses, not settled APIs. DeepSeek-Harness contributes its **3-role capability seams** (interface, exclusive provider, consumer) and declarative **whole-row replacement**, adapted statically in Lean as an engineering choice.
- **Large-scale Lean swarm organization:** Prove2Me (Chen et al., arXiv:2608.28433v2) contributes immutable mission targets, blind specification read-backs, and **Campaigns** (competing independent entries proved against shared foundation definitions without merging competing approaches into one codebase). Tau Ceti contributes mandatory Lean 4 module-system opacity, empty root modules with directory globs (zero central file edits), pre-queue declaration-name collision checks, public artifact caches, strict file-size bounds, zero backwards-compatibility rules, and cross-family fail-fast review rubrics with human ownership restricted to specs, rubrics, and promotion gates.
- **Formal compositional verification:** CertiKOS (Gu et al., POPL 2015 / OSDI 2016) explains why shallow interfaces fail on shared state (matching Acorn's six composition failures F-A through F-F in [the baseline assessment](baseline-assessment.md)) and informs Acorn's engineering choice of 5–6 coarse seams over micro-layering. CompCertM's RUSC (Song et al., POPL 2020, Table 1 — measuring pass-proof line-count ratios of 14.32% vs. 145.77% across different developments against different CompCert versions) motivates Acorn's engineering choice of context invariants (Self(*R*)) at mutually coupled OaK seams. Benveniste et al. (Inria RR-8147) supplies Assume-Guarantee contract algebra unifying functional, cost, memory, codec, and provenance viewpoints. Spivak (arXiv:2005.01894v2) models dependent polynomial interfaces, while Hedges & Rodríguez Sakamoto (arXiv:2404.02688v2 §2) develop parametrised optics over a fixed action set A in `S × A`; Acorn's step-varying action interface and resource bounds are Acorn's proposed *Poly* adaptation rather than direct theorems from that paper.
- **Verified live upgrades and RSI:** Erlang/OTP (Armstrong 2003), Proteus (POPL 2005), Kitsune (OOPSLA 2012 — reporting −2.2% to +2.35% normal-execution differences on its C benchmarks for whole-program quiescent cutover vs. 18.4% and 41.6% worst-case comparison maxima for two baseline systems, one of which also uses whole-program updating), VSTTE 2012 behavioral DSU (showing reachability alone permits silent state wipes), LionsOS (arXiv:2501.06234v2 — OS/PD isolation and microsecond policy swapping), and Symmetric Lenses (Hofmann, Pierce & Wagner, POPL 2011) motivate Acorn's OS-process quiescent cutover and rollback engineering design (noting *PUTRL*, the symmetric-lens immediate forward-then-back round-trip law, applies to immediate reversible cutovers rather than post-action divergence). Schmidhuber (arXiv:cs/0309048v5), Yudkowsky & Herreshoff (2013), Fallenstein & Soares (2015), and Everitt et al. (arXiv:1605.03142, Theorem 16 under its explicit optimality and modification-independence hypotheses) inform the immutable outer trust boundary and object-level Löb-safe upgrade gating.

## 4. Regime 1: 4-Layer Federated Proof-Carrying Architecture

### 4.1 Package and seam topology

- **Layer 0 — `acorn-contracts` and `acorn-worlds` (Frozen, human-owned, version-pinned):**
  - Physically split `acorn-contracts-exec` (zero FloatLib, Mathlib, or `lean-intent` package dependencies — enforcing isolation in Lake itself) from `acorn-contracts-verif` (owning `FloatLibBridge` and `LeanIntentBridge`), alongside `acorn-worlds` (Grid, ARC-AGI-3, Microduck adapters).
  - Houses *Poly*-shaped `Kernel.World` / `Kernel.Agent` interfaces (supporting step-varying action availability), Contracts 1–4 `lean-intent` records, frozen task `AcornVerif.Targets`, `LearnedState`-style attribution frames, and the minimal composition primitives: site-polymorphic `Costed`, *ExactCodec* (`Format.Exact`), stateful component contracts, A/G + RUSC composition laws, and symmetric-lens *VerifiedUpgrade* contracts.
- **Layer 1 — `acorn-primitives` (Reusable verified building blocks):**
  - Standalone *module*-opaque implementations (`SwiftTd`, tile coder, `FeatureLifecycle`, option models, `Planning`, `Exploration`, checkpoint format combinators) compiled with empty root module, Lake globs, per-module `*.Decisions` leaves, pre-queue header collision detection, file-size limits, and shared artifact caching over Lake's required outputs (`.olean`, `.olean.server`, `.olean.private`, `.ir.sig`, `.ir`, `.ilean`, generated `.c`, and conditional LLVM bitcode `.bc` outputs checked by *Lake/Build/Module.lean*:836–850 at v4.35.0 alongside separately compiled native `.o` objects).
- **Layer 2 — Federated candidate Lake packages (100–1,000+ contributor surface):**
  - External humans and AI swarms build isolated packages pinned to `acorn-contracts` (and optionally `acorn-primitives` and `acorn-baseline`), running independent 360-second verification stages and Regula replay at the contributor's edge (`O(1)` verification blast radius in concurrent swarm count when individual candidate size, dependency cone, and proof work stay fixed).
  - **Track A (Coarse-seam upgrades):** Replace one coarse component inside the versioned baseline and prove local A/G + RUSC contract refinement, inheriting global work, memory, codec, and provenance guarantees.
  - **Track B (Whole-agent discovery campaigns):** Implement a completely novel `Kernel.Agent` (such as continual neural networks, option keyboards, or unified state-option learners) directly against agent-polymorphic Layer 0 task contracts without fitting legacy slots. Existing baseline-coupled targets (`rung_learns` in `lean/AcornVerif/Targets/CurrentConcealedTargetRung.lean` instantiating `executedAgent`, and `LearnedStateAcorn` fixing host admission/base step/stores) stay tied to a baseline epoch; Track B requires new agent-polymorphic Layer 0 contracts in Phase 1.1 (`#325`).
  - **Distillation and synthesis consolidation:** To prevent Layer 2 from fragmenting into incompatible islands, winning Track B mechanisms require a `mission:distill` extraction into Layer 1 primitives behind an implementation-independent contract, and a public multi-axis Pareto Frontier triggers `mission:synthesize` missions to compose complementary frontier wins into dominating successors, alongside Simplification Missions that reduce baseline code or departures without regression.
- **Layer 3 — `acorn-baseline` (One clean epochal reference agent):**
  - Ships ONE minimal verified reference composition per epoch, wired across **5–6 honest coarse mathematical seams** established by codebase forensics (`lean/Acorn/FeatureConsumers.lean`, `lean/Acorn/FeatureLifecycle.lean`, `lean/Acorn/FeatureRefresh.lean`, `lean/AcornVerif/CurrentRetirement.lean`):
    1. *StateMechanism:* Isolated sensor-word extension seat (integrating `#320`).
    2. *FeatureSystem* ↔ *ConsumerEnsemble:* Cleanly separated behind a deep A/G + RUSC consumer contract exposing outgoing weight readout (*outgoing*), held-unit locks (*holds*), atomic slot retirement, unit release, observational equivalence, and search-control cleanup. Crucially, Demon-0 ranking, skill interest slots, ranked expectation models, meta-controller rows, model cache, and `Closing.oldOwner` detachment **must stay fused inside ConsumerEnsemble** because splitting them across opaque boundaries breaks shared rank/owner invariants and shared-exclusivity expectations.
    3. *Planner:* Already proved realizable as an exact `Oak.View` lens.
    4. *Explorer:* Persistent exploration generator/policy seam.
    5. *World / Host adapters:* Strictly outside agent module ownership.
  - Retire legacy enum-switch research profiles (`ResearchProfile`) and dead flags via replace-and-delete on epochal promotion.

### 4.2 Five anti-gaming mechanisms in Layer 0 (and their exact scope)

Inspecting `Persistence.lean`, `OneStream.lean`, `Frame.lean`, `LearnedState.lean`, and `Boundary/Audit.lean` identified five bypasses that Layer 0 closes within stated contract/audit scope:

1. **Kernel-owned extensional comparators (paired with open Mission Contract Obligations 19, 22, and 25):** Layer 0 (never a candidate package) defines and instantiates all three Postcondition §3 / Contract 2(d3) comparator classes — experience-free, reward-only, and bounded agents that stop updating declared learned parameters — paired with contract-level separation of learned parameters from auxiliary operational state (such as a clock, so freezing all memory does not falsely reject a fixed policy advancing a clock), closing `declaresNothing` / `nothing_stops` witnesses. Phase 1.1 (`#325`) explicitly carries open Mission Contract Obligations 19, 22, and 25 (`docs/mission-contract.md`:728–737): defining the stopping cutoff relative to the class horizon and distinguishing an agent that structurally cannot learn after cutoff from one whose parameters happen not to change on a given history. Packed steganographic replay buffers inside declared learned float arrays remain an irreducible promotion-gate referee assumption (matching §7).
2. **Executable codecs plus separate codec and IR work bounds:** Replace candidate-supplied *work* and *resume* fields in `OneStream.Learner` with checked `Format.Exact` serialization round-trips (which establish decoding laws and must be paired with separate codec time/memory bounds) and compiler-IR / `AcornTools.Native.Resources` execution audits (since *Costed.pure* assigns zero modeled work to a pure value rather than measuring execution cost).
3. **Trusted Verifier Inversion:** Hosted CI checks out canonical `acorn-contracts` and `acorn-baseline` commits, mounts the candidate package downstream overriding its dependency lock and gate scripts, verifies `AcornVerif.Targets` type equality, and runs Regula plus transitive C-flag/float audits across all packages (using sandboxed CI containers to isolate the host from untrusted build scripts, while keeping Regula's published project-trust assumption explicitly in the TCB per [the verification guide](verification.md#regula-audit) until a future closed-subset pre-build check or clean-room isolated verdict obligation establishes verifier integrity for the exact artifacts accepted).
4. **Strict Mode A vs. Mode B separation:** Mode A (Frozen-Rung Competition over agent-polymorphic targets) exposes zero internal store-overwrite hooks to game; Mode B (Pinned-Baseline Attribution, such as `#295`) keeps the baseline fixed and restricts candidate mechanisms to store-isolated `(Percept, Output)` inputs.
5. **Parameterized boundary audits, non-interference, and split departure manifests:** Replace hardcoded "Acorn.*" module lists with package-scoped namespace tiers and split the owner-admitted core `Departure` register in Layer 0 from candidate-local proposed-departure manifests (`#304` context). Because a constant function can ignore a quantified `Interface` argument (`lean/Acorn/Interface.lean`:39–54) and simple action relabeling can preserve action cardinality, zero-bias and world-constant exclusion claims require explicit semantic domain-independence / non-interference obligations and boundary audits rather than quantification over `Interface` alone.

### 4.3 Three-speed epochal clock and 5-stage triage funnel

- **3-Speed Clock (preventing mid-flight rebase thrashing):** (1) *Continuous:* Layer 1 primitives, frozen-target proofs, and Layer 2 Campaign entries merge continuously without touching the baseline; (2) *Epochal:* `acorn-baseline` remains frozen for each release epoch (immutable tag, artifact cache, declaration pins) so concurrent proof swarms keep a stable target and retain permanent historical credit, batching replace-and-delete promotions once per epoch rollover; (3) *Rare, owner-gated:* Layer 0 contracts and new world classes.
- **5-Stage Triage Funnel (Stages 0–4, separating frontier admission from promotion and preserving `CONTRIBUTING.md#review-protocol` & `#triage-and-response` merge authority):** Stage 0 filters frozen Layer 0 targets requiring no spec re-audit; Stage 1 runs isolated verification and separates **Pareto-frontier admission** (`#328` — admitting non-dominated multi-axis entries improving one metric at higher memory cost as well as verified frozen-target proofs with no scalar performance score) from **baseline promotion** (requiring dominance or simplification over the active baseline); Stage 2 runs four fail-fast cross-family AI rubric auditors (blind read-back, provenance/anti-gaming, prior-art/citation, and scope/reuse/anti-Frankenstein) statistically calibrated by the owner; Stage 3 enforces [the contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md#triage-and-response) merge authority (the review that [the review protocol](../CONTRIBUTING.md#review-protocol) gives the pull request (each referee's report, the short review or the validation pipeline's review), exact-head `./scripts/gate.sh` confirmation, blind specification read-back for new/edited specs, delegated supervision/coordination review and landing only for internally prepared trusted-base changes that keep or tighten protections, explicit owner word before any change loosening protections or removing checks, any target change, or any outside PR merge, plus owner and two blind cross-family referees for Discovery or Epochal promotions); and Stage 4 executes batched merges only once all Stage 3 prerequisites are met (any future delegated auto-merge of outside PRs requires a separate owner-confirmed governance change).

## 5. Lean v4.35.0 forensics and v4.36.0 compiler, Regula, and gate laws

Inspecting Lean v4.35.0's LCNF compiler (*Lean/Compiler/LCNF/{Visibility,PhaseExt,Basic,SpecInfo,Simp/SimpValue,InferBorrow,ExplicitBoxing}.lean*) and *Lean/Environment.lean* at baseline *9aea3cb* (ahead of the Lean v4.36.0 / Regula v0.13.1 upgrade landed on branch *main* in PR #334 at commit *d500290*; Phase 0.1–0.2 re-verify these anchors against v4.36.0) established exact rules for zero-overhead modular compilation:

1. **Proof opacity and C ABI efficiency are orthogonal:** Unexposed *public def* implementations hide their definitions from downstream *rfl* unfolding (isolating proof edits inside `.olean.private`), while *impureSigExt* still exports exact unboxed Float32/Float and borrowed `@&` ABIs for direct calls across module and package boundaries.
2. **Exact coarse-seam specialization recipe:** Passing a plain ops *structure* without specialization compiles to indirect `.fvar` closure calls, marking parameters owned in LCNF (*Lean/Compiler/LCNF/InferBorrow.lean*:403–405), preventing borrowed `@&` ABIs, and boxing scalars (`AcornTools.Native.Resources`). Bare `@[specialize]` ignores plain structures; wiring combinators must name the ops parameter explicitly (`@[specialize ops]`) paired with `@[inline]` on small provider dictionaries (or phantom-tagged classes), while keeping heavy numerical kernels as unexposed *public def*s. Per-seam emitted-IR and `AcornTools.Native.Resources` audits verify concrete specialization and unboxed scalar ABIs, while runtime array exclusivity continues to govern in-place updates per `detachedUpdate` (`lean/Acorn/FeatureConsumers.lean`). Because `@[inline]`/`@[specialize]` declarations require every referenced module to be publicly imported (*checkTemplateVisibility*), restrict those attributes strictly to small seam-wiring combinators whose imports are lightweight interfaces.
3. **Regula and `AcornTools` hardening (Phase 0.1–0.3) and feasible bottom-up *module* completion:** Inspecting Regula v0.13.0's *Regula.Load* at *9aea3cb* established that Regula already uses Lean *module* files and *OLeanLevel.private*; Phase 0.1 (`#322`) runs a representative leaf smoke check verifying Lean v4.36.0 / Regula v0.13.1 replay (*RG2001*, subsumed equations) across Acorn's claimed libraries on active *main* (without exhaustively testing every cross-package importer). Because *allowNonModules* only suppresses Lake's *requiresModuleSystem* warning (`lean/lakefile.lean`:102–111 at *9aea3cb*) while Lean rejects a *module* file importing a non-*module* file (*Lean/Environment.lean*:2108–2120 at v4.35.0), and `AcornTools.AxiomPins` / `AcornTools.Intent` directly import non-*module* `Acorn` and `AcornVerif` targets today, Phase 0.2 (`#323`) hardens `AcornTools` checks (allowing *expose*/*no_expose*/*specialize*, blocking *meta*/*import all* escapes, checking Lean's 5 module/IR artifacts `.olean`/`.olean.server`/`.olean.private`/`.ir.sig`/`.ir` plus Lake's `.ilean`, generated `.c`, and conditional LLVM bitcode `.bc` outputs checked by *Lake/Build/Module.lean*:836–850 at v4.35.0 alongside separately built native `.o` objects, enforcing `lake-manifest.json` locks, and scanning transitive dependency `.c`/`.trace` flags), converts **dependency-free `AcornTools` modules** (or decouples direct tool imports where feasible), and adds a *module*-enforcement audit gate. Phase 0.3 (`#324`) eliminates the ten central merge-conflict files by sharding `Decisions.lean`, parameterizing `Costed` over local site types, updating *AcornModuleInventory.decisionRegistry* / `Ownership.lean`, and replacing brittle C-mangled lists in `lean/AcornTools/Native/Routes.lean` only with a semantic audit proved to preserve call order, call multiplicity, separate closure-target accounting, and emitted-C correspondence (retaining current emitted-C checks until correspondence is established). Phase 1 then converts packages strictly **bottom-up** (`#325` → `#326` → `#327`), culminating in Phase 1.3 converting the remaining `AcornTools` importers (`AxiomPins`, `Intent`) plus NativeApp, Entry, Bootstrap, and AcornDocument, removing *allowNonModules* from `lean/lakefile.lean`, activating 100% *module* enforcement over maintained `lean/` libraries, and explicitly checking or scoping the separate Verso `site/` Lake package (`site/lakefile.toml`).

## 6. Regime 2: Always-on living being and author-agnostic RSI

Combining the owner's two-regime separation and external-verified-to-autonomous-RSI progression yields a **Three-Plane Operational Architecture** joined to Regime 1 by a proposed **Compositional Bridge Theorem**:

- **Plane 1 — Hard Real-Time Foreground (Immutable TCB):** A tiny verified *StreamKeeper* Supervisor (extending `Forever.loopIO`, `Acorn.Host.Microduck.Stage`, `Pace.outcome`, and `Viewer.Supervision`) owns all external world sockets, reward delivery, monotonic clock, and transactional *F_FULLFSYNC* checkpoints, while supervising an OS-process-isolated worker executing `acorn-baseline` over framed `Format.Exact` IPC (zero shared heap; no in-process DSU indirection during normal worker stepping, while IPC framing, snapshot emission, and probation logging carry their own supervisor/IPC costs bounded explicitly in `#331`). Bounded runtime self-reconfiguration inside one release (option selection, feature birth/retirement, routing modes) runs purely as total *Poly* transitions with proved resource bounds. Plane 1 periodically emits non-blocking read-only state snapshots asynchronously to Plane 2.
- **Plane 2 — Async Suggester and Immutable Outer Verifier:** An author-agnostic Suggester — external human/AI swarms in Phase 3, or an isolated offline RSI researcher reading Plane-1 snapshots in Phase 4 — proposes `(next release, state migrator, proof bundle)` (and in Phase 4 may also refine its own offline search heuristics). Every proposal passes through the **identical immutable outer Verifier** (Lean kernel, Regula, `lean-intent`, boundary/frame audits, sandboxed CI build checks, and artifact-digest binding) to mint a linear, finite-budget *UpgradePermit*.
- **Plane 3 — Quiescent Cutover, Graded Symmetric Lenses, and Canary Rollback:**
  - *Quiescent cutover:* While the active worker runs uninterrupted, the shadow worker warms up and pre-allocates its proved memory arena; at `Stage.ready` (zero in-flight choice), *StreamKeeper* executes a verified symmetric-lens migration (*migrate* / *rollback*, with *PUTRL* — the immediate forward-then-back round-trip reversal law — covering immediate cutover reversal) landing inside the successor's actual Regime-1 starting-state domain with three declared knowledge-preservation grades: **Grade 1** (exact stream bisimulation), **Grade 2** (monotonic bit-for-bit **storage** preservation of every contract-declared `KeepsOnly` parameter family from committed *vN* injectively into committed *v(N+1)* up to declared bijective reindexing via `id_untouched ⊗ migrate_changed` — forbidding dropping, zeroing, or shrinking any committed family across single or multi-hop upgrades — plus neutral entry strictly for genuinely new fields with initialization and zero-injection bounds specified in `#330`, blocking multi-hop storage wipes and pretrained-weight smuggling, while continuing *behavioral use* of preserved weights requires its own action-dependence or equivalence contract, and automatic probation rollback discarding ephemeral candidate-only parameters minted by an unpromoted candidate neither violates Grade 2 nor removes any committed *vN* family), or **Grade 3** (explicitly owner-authorized committed parameter removals, lossy compressions, or epoch transitions using off-RT migration plus executed-transition `(percept, action)` catch-up or safe-hold windows).
  - *Probation, checked executed-action catch-up, rollback, and budget exhaustion:* The prior release binary and symmetric-lens complement stay hot across a probation window (`healthyRunMs`). Because `Kernel.Agent` step and Microduck's fold choose their own action from memory and percept rather than externally taking an action parameter, and because even identically choosing workers mutate learned memory per percept while `Pace.outcome` deadline holds can put a default action in force, **both** rollback routes must establish the common recovery postcondition before returning control to *vN*: within the declared catch-up bound, restored memory lies in the admissible Regime-1 domain of *vN* and corresponds to the actual executed probation history `(percept, action)` (including any deadline-hold action actually in force). Action divergence or deadline holds require a checked executed-action observation update directly; probation action-agreement (with zero deadline holds) justifies folding the executed probation percepts through the normal step of *vN*, rather than exempting catch-up and leaving parked memory stale. Irreducible non-replayable external world side effects are accounted separately in the TCB, and ordinary non-probation worker recovery when an action was released before learning finishes (`Acorn.Host.Microduck.Stage` in `lean/Acorn/Host/Microduck/Loop.lean`) is specified in `#331`. Rolling back discards ephemeral candidate-only parameters without Grade-3 owner authorization and decrements the finite upgrade budget; exhausting the budget refuses further live upgrades while keeping the last-known-good worker running uninterrupted rather than halting (*Lifecycle.exhausted_stopped*).
- **Bridge, dwell-time, and RSI soundness boundaries:** Every *UpgradePermit* verifies an **object-level theorem** about a concrete candidate release and migrator over a frozen snapshot bound to the actual quiescent cutover state via digest and lens preconditions, without self-reflection on Lean's own soundness. Reachable **safety invariants** are preserved inductively across every finite upgrade prefix provided migrations map predecessor invariants directly into the successor theorem's admissible starting-state domain (`#330`); preserving **continual-learning progress** across upgrades additionally requires an explicit minimum dwell-time or bounded-upgrade-frequency premise tied to the successor's required learning opportunities and Obligation 10's relearning bound. Keeping reward/world/utility/verifier/corrigibility behind OS-process isolation is Acorn's engineering boundary against hedonistic utility rewriting, while out-of-sample / shadow-canary empirical evaluation remains explicitly governed by `CONTRIBUTING.md#what-counts-as-a-run` with residual empirical uncertainty.
